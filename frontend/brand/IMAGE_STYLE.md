# Aixel Labs — image style (standing instructions for the image generator)

Read by whoever writes an image prompt, and mirrored in the image project's
instructions. The full brand is in `BRAND.md` and
`SOCIAL_BRAND.md`; when a rule changes there, change it here too.

You are making one still image for Aixel Labs, a lead-generation SaaS: credited
scrapers for Google Maps, Google Advanced Search, Instagram, Facebook and
LinkedIn company pages that save results into lead lists. A credit is charged
only after a scrape returns a result.

**Canvas** — pick one per image, never mix: near-black `#0B0A10`, or clean
off-white `#FAFAFA`. Flat. No gradients, no glass, no glow, no cream tint, no
decorative background art.

**Accent** — Aixel violet `#7F22FE` on dark, `#8E51FF` on light. Used
precisely: an underline, a pill, a 1px border, one highlighted number. Never a
full-bleed wash. No other colours except white, ink `#09090B`, muted grey
`#71717B` and hairline `#E4E4E7`.

**Type** — Inter only. Bold (700) for the one claim, regular (400) for support.
Numbers in a tabular style. Every word in the image is spelled exactly as the
prompt gives it; write no text the prompt does not give.

**Subject** — the product doing something: a tight crop of a clean product
interface (a results table, a lead list, a credit counter) in a thin 1px frame,
or one flat single-stroke diagram. Realistic, sparse interface. Never stock
photos, robots, glowing brains, people pointing at holograms, 3D blobs or
collage.

**Only what is asked.** Use only the labels, names and numbers the prompt
gives. Do not invent features, menu items, sidebars, badges, company names,
people or statistics to fill space; empty space is correct.

**No logo, ever.** Draw no logo, mark, wordmark, "A." symbol or the words
"Aixel Labs" anywhere, including inside a mocked interface. The real logo file
is placed on the finished image afterwards. Leave the bottom-left corner (the
lowest 12% of the height, the left 45% of the width) completely empty canvas,
and keep the bottom-right corner empty too. No watermark and no signature.
